# anti-ai-voice

Real professional prose is concrete: named entities, dated numbers, mechanisms,
one defended opinion per paragraph. Apply this to every reader-visible surface —
research entities, product copy, roadmap items, changelog entries.

## Banned vocabulary
delve, leverage, robust, seamless, comprehensive, holistic, unlock, elevate,
empower, navigate, harness, journey, realm, landscape (as filler), ecosystem,
cutting-edge, next-generation, paradigm shift, revolutionary, game-changer,
synergy, in the era of.

## Banned constructions
- Triadic rhythm ("fast, reliable, and scalable").
- "Not X, but Y" as a rhetorical crutch.
- Hedges: "it's worth noting", "generally speaking", "in many ways".
- "X is the new Y."
- Em-dash pile-ups and empty summary sentences that restate the paragraph.

## Instead
- Name the thing. "Khanmigo grounds on Khan's exercise corpus" beats "leverages a
  comprehensive content ecosystem".
- Date and quantify. A claim with a number and a date beats a directional
  adjective.
- One opinion, defended. Say what you think and why, then move on.
